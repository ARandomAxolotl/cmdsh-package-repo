{yellow}NAME{reset}
    {cyan}grep{reset} - Search for patterns in text or files with highlighting.

{yellow}SYNOPSIS{reset}
    {cyan}grep{reset} {green}<pattern>{reset} [{green}file{reset}]

{yellow}DESCRIPTION{reset}
    Searches for the specified {green}<pattern>{reset} using findstr.
    Matches are highlighted in {red}bright red{reset} in the output.
    If [{green}file{reset}] is omitted, it reads from standard input.
