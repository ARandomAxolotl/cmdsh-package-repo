{yellow}NAME{reset}
    {cyan}view{reset} - A stylized pager for text files.

{yellow}SYNOPSIS{reset}
    {cyan}view{reset} {green}<filename>{reset}

{yellow}DESCRIPTION{reset}
    A wrapper for {cyan}cat{reset} that automatically enables line 
    numbering and pipes output through {cyan}more{reset} for easy 
    reading of long files.

    Inherits all {magenta}color tag{reset} parsing from the {cyan}cat{reset} engine.

{yellow}DEPENDENCIES{reset}
    {cyan}cat{reset} v2 or higher.
