{yellow}NAME{reset}
    {cyan}rm{reset} - Remove files or directories.

{yellow}SYNOPSIS{reset}
    {cyan}rm{reset} [{magenta}-rf{reset}] [{magenta}--no-preserve-root{reset}] {green}<target>{reset}

{yellow}OPTIONS{reset}
    {magenta}-rf{reset}                 Force deletion of files or recursive deletion of folders.
    {magenta}--no-preserve-root{reset}  Allows operations on 'C:\' ({red}Dangerous{reset}).

{yellow}DESCRIPTION{reset}
    Deletes the specified target.
    Without {magenta}-rf{reset}, it uses standard del or rd commands.
