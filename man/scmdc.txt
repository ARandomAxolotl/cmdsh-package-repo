{yellow}NAME{reset}
    {cyan}scmdc{reset} - Secure CMD Compiler.

{yellow}SYNOPSIS{reset}
    {cyan}scmdc{reset} [{magenta}-s{reset}] [{magenta}-d{reset}] [{magenta}-v{reset}] [{magenta}-b{reset}] {green}<filename>{reset}

{yellow}OPTIONS{reset}
    {magenta}-s{reset}    Silent mode; suppresses output.
    {magenta}-d{reset}    Debug mode; shows line-by-line processing.
    {magenta}-v{reset}    Verbose mode; turns on echo.
    {magenta}-b{reset}    Bat mode; processes .sbat into .bat files.

{yellow}DESCRIPTION{reset}
    Compiles .scmd files into .cmd.
    It checks for "suspect" commands and paths defined in the 
    config, disabling any blocked lines it encounters.
